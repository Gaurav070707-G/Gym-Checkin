How to get the link
  1. Open the Google Maps app and search for your gym.
  2. Open the gym's own page (with its name and photos), tap Share, then Copy link.
  3. Paste the link above and tap Find on map.
Gym not listed on Maps? Long-press the gym's exact spot on the map, copy the coordinates shown at the top (e.g. 19.0760, 72.8777) and paste them.
